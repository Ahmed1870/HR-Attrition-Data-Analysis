# Analysis Notes

## Primary KPI

Attrition Rate = Attrited Employees / Total Employees

The denominator changes with the active filter context in Power BI.

## Interpretation

The strongest intended signals are overtime, commute distance, and income level. These should be treated as investigation priorities rather than proof of causation.

A sensible real-world next step would be to test whether workload, compensation, commute, and manager/team effects remain significant after controlling for age, tenure, department, and job role.
